# Data license

The data files of this repository are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
license (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). They are:

- `sim3/results/` (raw simulation results, runs of the two aborted launches, checker verdicts);
- the raw outputs in `sim3/experiments/` (`*.txt`, `*.json`, `*.stats`, `*.stuck`, `*.err`);
- `bench3/fault_reach.csv`;
- `analysis3/*.json`;
- `validation/results/`, `validation/nonatomic/*.txt`, `validation/nonatomic/summary.json`;
- `hw/*.txt`, `hw/*.log` and `hw/synth_context.json` (synthesis reports and logs, functional check, sensitivity of
  the areas to the synthesis context);
- `paper3/gen/` and `paper3/generated_values.json` (generated tables and values).

Attribution: cite the article "Routing in Three-Dimensional Networks-on-Chip: A Critical Survey and a Verified
Multi-Dimensional Evaluation" (A. Mesellem), see `CITATION.cff`.
